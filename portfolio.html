About 200 conversations with manufacturers, turned into a product and a brand the company could raise on.

Galvant builds AI for the back office of manufacturing companies. I joined ahead of its Series A to design what customers and investors would see: the product interface, the full brand, the marketing site, the pitch deck, and the kit the team takes to conferences.

The design work ran alongside discovery. I spoke with plant general managers, contract manufacturers, and finance leads in about ten countries, from the U.S., Mexico, and Argentina to Europe, Singapore, and Thailand, and brought what I heard back into product and positioning decisions.

  • ~200discovery conversations
  • $100K+pipeline from 5 qualified opportunities
  • ~10countries across the Americas, Europe, and Asia

What I designed

  1. Full brand. The complete identity for the company's rename to Galvant: serif display type paired with a monospace, on a cream, orange, and plum palette, applied across every surface below.
  2. Product UI. The interface for four product lines: Intelligence, Sales, Procurement, and Financial.
  3. Site and deck. The marketing site at galvant.ai and the pitch deck for the Series A raise.
  4. Conference kit. The banner, business cards, and materials the team carries to industry conferences.

Draft note, remove before publishing. Still to add: two findings from the discovery conversations and the design decision each one changed, plus one option you rejected and why.

A waitlist of 5,000 for a headset nobody could try yet.

Epiphany paired a wearable headset with an AI browser to help people get into deep focus on demand. As co-founder and COO I owned how the product was positioned and launched before it existed in anyone's hands.

That meant designing the story as much as the screens: the positioning, the brand, and the launch page that carried the waitlist. Alongside it I ran day-to-day operations across engineering, neuroscience, and design, from prototype through pilots and user testing.

  • 5,000people on the pre-launch waitlist
  • $10Kraised from angels
  • 3teams: engineering, neuroscience, design

The product

  1. A performance headset. Wearable neurotech that primes the brain for focus before a work session starts.
  2. An AI browser. Blocks distractions, keeps you on task, and guides the session while you work.
  3. Focus tracking. Shows focus trends and flow states over time, so the next session can be better than the last.

What I designed

  1. Positioning. One promise, deep focus on demand, carried by three product pillars: the headset, the AI browser, and focus tracking.
  2. Launch page and waitlist. The pre-launch site and sign-up flow that grew the waitlist to 5,000.
  3. Pilots and user testing. Early pilot programs, with feedback routed back to the engineering and neuroscience teams.

Recognition

  • Stanford Accelerator for Edtech Impact Summit Winner, 2025
  • India Ascends Lightspeed 2026 Finalist
  • Pear FFC Fellow
  • Foundress Fellow

Where it stands

I put Epiphany on pause in July 2026. The waitlist, the positioning, and what we learned from pilots are still there to build on.
